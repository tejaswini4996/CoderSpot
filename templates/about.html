WHY THIS EXISTS

Nobody misses a deadline they can see coming.

The problem

Every year, dozens of coding fellowships, bootcamps, and open source programs open and close applications on their own schedule — scattered across different websites, newsletters, and social feeds. Great programs get missed simply because nobody was watching the calendar that week.

The scoreboard

CoderSpot puts the whole year on one board: women-focused programs, men-focused programs, and open-to-all programs, side by side, sorted by month. Check it like you'd check a season schedule — see what's coming up, and don't let the window close on you.

Keeping it current

This is a starter template — the events, dates, and links live in one plain file (data/events.py) so they're easy to update each year as programs announce new rounds. If you're running this site, treat that file as your yearly to-do: confirm dates, swap in new programs, and retire ones that have shut down.
